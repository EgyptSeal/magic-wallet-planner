How valuation works

For “How many grams can I buy?” the app uses the best available dealer price to buy gold. For portfolio/liquidation value it uses the best available dealer buy-back price. Prices are indicative and can change during the day.

Market data source: Daleelak public Egypt price feed (licensed dealers/banks). Live refresh requires internet.

ℹ️ About Magic Wallet Planner

Developer: Hossam Hegazi

This app helps you record money, understand cash flow, plan future spending, build savings, work toward goals and create practical debt-payoff ideas.

Permissions: Camera is used only when you scan a receipt/invoice. Photo library is used only when you choose a receipt or background image. Microphone is used only when you activate voice entry. Download/share access is used only when you export or share your backup. No permission is needed for normal budgeting and planning.

Your working finance data stays in this browser/device unless you choose to export or share it.

Privacy

No account login or cloud database is required. Your working data remains in this browser's localStorage unless you choose to export it.
